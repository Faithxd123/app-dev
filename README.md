# My Favorite Series: Dark

> A German mystery series where the disappearance of a child exposes the secrets of four families and a time loop that connects generations.

## Overview

*Dark* is a German science fiction thriller created by **Baran bo Odar** and **Jantje Friese**. It was Netflix's first German original series and ran from 2017 to 2020. The story is set in the small town of **Winden**, where time does not move in a ~~straight line~~ **cycle**.

## The Four Families

| Family | Key Character | Connection to the Mystery |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Kahnwald | Jonas | The main character who travels through time |
| Nielsen | Ulrich | A police officer searching for his missing son |
| Doppler | Charlotte | The police chief with a hidden past |
| Tiedemann | Claudia | The head of the nuclear power plant |
